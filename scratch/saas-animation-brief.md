# Quvexa Restro — SaaS Animation Brief

## Brand Name
**Quvexa Restro** (by Quvexa Tech)

## Headline
**"Your restaurant, fully online — in one scan."**

### Subheadline
QR ordering, live kitchen tracking, staff logins, and real online payments — one system, zero paperwork.

## Product / Feature List
1. **QR Ordering** — Customer scans table QR, browses menu, orders in seconds
2. **Live Order Tracking** — Kitchen to table, real-time status for customer and staff
3. **Staff Roles** — Separate logins for Owner, Manager, Waiter, Cook — no shared passwords
4. **Real Online Payments** — UPI, Card via Razorpay, straight to the restaurant's own account
5. **Own Branded Domain** — restaurantname.com, fully theirs
6. **Financial Year Reports** — Clean, separated yearly analytics — revenue, orders, top items
7. **WhatsApp Customer CRM** — Order history, bulk messaging, customer insights
8. **Multi-Restaurant Ready** — One owner, unlimited outlets, isolated data per restaurant
